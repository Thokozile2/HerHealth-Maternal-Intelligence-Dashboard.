HerHealth Matters: Maternal Health Intelligence Dashboard
Strategic Alignment: UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health & Well-being)
Target 3.1: Reduce global maternal mortality.
Target 3.4: Promote mental health and well-being in clinical settings.

Project Overview
HerHealth Matters is a predictive analytics solution designed to empower frontline healthcare workers with actionable maternal risk data. By analyzing a population of 1,100 patients, the dashboard identifies physiological and psychological drivers that contribute to high-risk outcomes.
As the dashboard designer of this initiative, I have developed this tool to bridge the gap between raw biometric data and clinical intervention strategies.

The Big Discovery: The 4.75x Multiplier
The standout feature of this project is the AI-driven Risk Analysis. While traditional vitals (BMI, Blood Pressure) are important, our machine learning model identified Mental Health as the primary driver of maternal risk in this dataset:
 a) Mental Health Priority: Increases high-risk probability by 4.75x.
 b) Previous Complications: Increases risk likelihood by 3.46x.
 c)Biometric Thresholds: Systolic BP >135 and BMI increases are secondary but significant drivers.

Dashboard Architecture
The report is structured into three specialized views:

A. HerHealth Overview: A macro-view of the 1,100-patient registry, showing that 40.5% are currently High Risk.

B. HerHealth Clinical Analysis: A deep-dive into the "Biometric Risk Quadrant" (BP vs. BMI) to identify patients requiring immediate specialist referral.

C. HerHealth AI Insights: An exploration of risk pathways, featuring a Decomposition Tree that identifies a 94.3% risk probability for the intersection of age (40-49) and mental health challenges.

Healthcare Utility (How to use this tool):
a) For Nurses: Use the Age Group Slicer to prioritize patients during high-volume clinic days.

b) For Midwives: Use the Clinical Analysis Quadrant to visually confirm if a patient’s vitals have crossed into the high-risk "Pink Zone."

c) For Specialists: Use the AI Risk Drivers to justify the integration of mental health support into standard prenatal care protocols.

Technical Details
Platform: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Data Volume: 1,100 Clinical Patient Records
Key Features: Power Query (Data Cleaning), DAX (Risk Measures), AI Key Influencers, and Decomposition Tree visuals.
